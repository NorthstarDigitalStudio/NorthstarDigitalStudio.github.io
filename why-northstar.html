Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers.

Here are some of the questions businesses commonly have before starting a website project with Northstar.

How much does a website cost?
Our introductory website packages start at $199, with larger projects starting at $399 and $599. Final pricing depends on the number of pages, content, functionality and overall project scope.
How long does it take to build a website?
Project timelines depend on the size and complexity of the website and how quickly the necessary content is provided. Smaller websites can generally move faster than larger, more customized projects.
Do I need to provide all of the content?
Not necessarily. We can work with the information, images and materials you already have and help structure the website around your business. If additional content is needed, we'll discuss what makes sense for your project.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We can modernize an outdated website by improving its design, structure, messaging, mobile experience and calls to action while keeping the focus on your business goals.
Do you handle domains and hosting?
Domain registration and hosting can be handled separately depending on your preferences. We can discuss the options and help make sure your website is properly connected and accessible after launch.
Do you offer website maintenance?
Yes. Ongoing maintenance is available for businesses that need help keeping their website current. This can include text and image updates, new pages, promotions, basic SEO improvements and general website management.
Do you work with businesses remotely?
Yes. Website projects can be handled remotely through email and online communication, making it possible to work with businesses regardless of location.
What happens after I submit an inquiry?
We'll review the information you provide and follow up by email to discuss your business, project goals, scope and potential next steps. There is no obligation or commitment required just to start the conversation.
Do I have to know exactly what I want before contacting you?
No. You don't need to have the entire project figured out before reaching out. Tell us what your business does, what you currently have and what you'd like to improve, and we'll help determine what makes sense.
